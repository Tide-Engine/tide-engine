# Geometry package

## First version (2026-11-02)

`packages/geometry`, namespace `Geometry`: mesh building over a game's own vertex structs, in ordinary generic Tide with no C. The owner approved Claude's proposal on 2026-11-02 ("go with you recommendations"): a bundled package rather than built-ins, generic over `V` through generic functions and `V has <type> <field>` (`notes/language.md`, Generic functions and `has`), and `Weld` requiring `V`'s `==`. The shape list, defaults, clamps, names and the choices below are Claude's, Provisional. User docs: `docs/packages/geometry.md`.

What's built, and what code relies on:

- **The field contract is the mesh asset's** (`docs/spec.md`, Mesh assets): `position` (`float3`, or `float2` for 2D), `normal` (`float3`), `uv` (`float2`), `tangent` (`float4`, or `float3`). `MakeVertex<V>` starts from `V { }`, so other fields keep the struct's declared defaults, and writes only what `V has`. A struct with no `position` is an error at the instantiation, with the call's note.
- **Shapes** (`Triangle`, `Quad`, `Polygon`, each also over `float2` corners; `Box`, `Plane`, `Sphere`, `Cylinder`, `Cone`, `Disc`) append vertices and indices and return the first vertex's index. Every one takes `float4x4 transform = float4x4.identity` last. `PlacementOf` turns it into a `Placement`: points and tangents through it, normals through its inverse transpose (or itself when its determinant is 0), and `mirrored` for a negative determinant, where `Face` writes each triangle's last two corners the other way round and `Moved` turns the tangent's `w`. An identity transform (`IsIdentity`, all 16 numbers) copies vertices as they are.
- **Conventions:** fronts clockwise (`normal = Cross(b - a, c - a)`), as Tide/Unity have them; uv (0, 0) at the top left; tangent `w` = the side `Cross(normal, tangent)` is on relative to the texture's v direction (`Handedness`), as the mesh importer's generated tangents (`engine/src/mesh_decode.c`, `make_tangents`). Box faces each have their four vertices, `up` as seen from outside fixed per face (+z for the top, -z for the bottom, +y for the sides). The sphere repeats its seam column and leaves out the zero-area triangles at its poles. Cone side normals lean by the radii's difference; an end with radius 0 has no disc.
- **Counts** of segments, rings and cells are clamped to [3 | 2 | 1, `MOST_STEPS` = 1024], so a bad number can't allocate a machine's memory. Tide's lists already make every index safe; the processing functions also skip a triangle with a corner outside the vertices (`Inside`) rather than read zeros.
- **`RecalculateNormals`**: area-weighted (unnormalized cross products summed in triangle order), then `Math.Normalize`; a vertex no triangle has gets zero.
- **`RecalculateTangents`** ports the importer's generation to `float`: per triangle the u and v directions from uv derivatives (sign of the determinant), each corner's contribution flattened against its normal and weighted by the corner angle (`CornerAngle`, `Math.Atan2`, or the sine below that), summed per side of `w`; `FlatTo` falls back to the least-aligned axis. The importer works in `double` with C's `atan2` and splits vertices whose sides disagree; the package keeps the vertex count and writes the +1 side's sum. So results are close to an imported mesh's, not bit-identical.
- **`Weld`** hashes `Math.Hash(position + 0)` (so `-0` is `0`) into chains (`Map<int, int>` heads, a `next` list) and compares candidates with `V`'s `==`; the first of equal vertices keeps its place. **`WeldPositions`** compares positions only, exactly or within a tolerance through 27 neighbouring cells of that size, keeping the first vertex's other fields. **`RemoveUnused`** drops triangles outside the vertices or with a repeated corner, then unused vertices, keeping order. **`BoundsOf`** returns `Bounds?` (`min`, `max`, `Center()`, `Size()`, `Extents()`, `Contains`), z = 0 for 2D. `Append` adds another mesh's lists through a transform; `TransformMesh` moves a mesh in place (`Transform` is a reserved name).
- **Performance** (2026-11-02, release flags, Windows, a 256 x 256 plane: 66,049 vertices, 393,216 indices, best of 15 ticks; this machine was noisy, ±30%): `Plane` 7.8 ms against the same loop written in Tide 7.7; `RecalculateNormals` 6.3 against 6.2 by hand; `RecalculateTangents` 40; `WeldPositions` 19; 4,096 transformed boxes 6.4. The package is the handwritten Tide's speed. Two things learned on the way: a helper called per element (`PositionOf(vertices[a])`, `AddTangent(list, ...)`) cost 40% and more, because each call starts its own list cache, so the hot loops read fields where they are; and **handwritten Tide over a 66k-element world list is ~14x plain C** (normals: 6.2 ms against 0.45 ms over arrays of the same 64-byte struct). That gap is the list runtime's (chunked big lists, a one-run `tide_list_cache` that misses on the index-scattered reads a mesh makes, and `tide_list_add` per append), not the generics or the package: reported to the owner as a performance gap, not changed here.
- **Determinism:** only Tide math in a fixed order. `geometry_shapes_are_the_same_bits_everywhere` pins an FNV hash of the sphere, cone, recalculated ground and welded box (2466882126) on native; CI's other platforms and the web build check it agrees.
- **Tests:** `tide_geometry` (`packages/geometry/test/game.tide`, `game_test.c`): counts, outward fronts against normals, unit and orthogonal frames, tangents along the texture's u with `w` its v side, struct defaults kept, 2D vertices, mirrored `Append` with bounds, `Weld` keeping a box's 24 and `WeldPositions` making 8 with smooth normals, recalculated ground frames, `RemoveUnused`, empty bounds, and the hash.

Remaining gaps: an `Append` overload taking a `Mesh<V>` (inference through `Mesh<V>` isn't built; `rock.vertices, rock.indices` works), tangent seam splitting as an option, a capsule/torus, uv2, and anything using the GPU.
