Make a small dodging game at 640×360. The player is a square near the bottom that moves left and right with the arrow keys or A/D and cannot leave the screen. Hazards fall from random positions along the top; a new one appears every half second, and they fall faster the longer the run lasts. Hazards that leave the bottom disappear. Touching a hazard ends the run: everything stops and the screen shows how many seconds the player survived. Space starts a new run. Show the current survival time while playing. Simple shapes only, no external assets.
