# CRACKED

The owner asked on 2026-10-11 for a port of their Unity game CRACKED
(`D:\wkspaces\unity\riten\Purrguelike-lfs`, on Steam as app 3917010) to Tide,
as a prototype on the admin page with a public link: players who join a match
through the `rooms` package, maps that rotate, upgrades and the rest, without
the modding scene. `prototypes/cracked/` is it. In the same request the owner
had every other prototype but Two Forks deleted (`notes/private-prototypes.md`).

`prototypes/cracked/DESIGN.md` says what each part was read from in the
Unity project, where the port differs and why, which of the Unity game's
oddities are kept, and what isn't ported. `README.md` there has the controls
and how to build it, and `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md` where each asset came from.
This note is what code relies on, the tests, and what isn't checked.

No engine, compiler or package change was made. The engine gaps the game
works around are listed at the end, for the owner.

## The match

`game.tide` is the match, at 30 Hz as the Unity game's, for up to eight
players (4v4) with host migration. `Arena` is one singleton with everything
in it: eight `Seat`s (a struct each: PlayerMovement, ShooterBase, PlayerShield
and PlayerHealth in one), the bullets, clouds, the map's pieces, movers and
blocks, each seat's modifiers and effects, the cards taken, the teams' scores
and the round's state. The only entities are a `Participant` for each player,
which ties their input to a seat.

- **One system simulates it, `Tick`, in a fixed order**: what moves in the
  map, then each seat (bot's input, `Shooter`, `Movement`, `Shield`,
  `Health`), bullets, clouds, every body's step (`Integrate`), players apart
  from each other, jump pads, and the round's state. That's the order the
  Unity game has: every `Simulate`, then PhysX's step. A force is a `push`
  (and a torque a `twist`) kept in the seat until `Integrate`.
- **A seat's fields are written where they are** (`arena.seats[s].health`),
  never through a copy taken out: a function that hurts another seat while a
  copy of the first is out would lose one of the two. `Bullets` is the one
  place a struct is taken out, changed and put back, as a bullet's own tick
  changes nothing of another bullet's.
- **Modifiers and effects are flat lists** with the seat in each entry
  (`bulletMods`, `shootMods`, `traits`), in the order cards were applied,
  which is the order they act in. `SpawnPlayer` clears a seat's and applies
  its cards again from `picks` (seat times 64 plus the card), as the Unity
  game applies upgrades at each spawn. `Effect` is a name of the engine's,
  so a player effect is a `Trait`.
- **Seats.** A seat's team is its number's parity. A player who joins has
  the first bot's seat of the team with fewer players, as it is, or that
  team's first free seat. `KeepTeams` gives a team with no player one bot
  and takes bots from a team that has players. A player who leaves frees
  their seat at once.
- **The dice are seeded in `Begin`** from `Random.seed`: left to the engine,
  a singleton's `Random` is seeded by where the singleton is among the
  program's, and a view's file adding a singleton changed which map came
  first.
- **Events** (`Fired`, `Hurt`, `Died`, `BulletHit` and the rest) are for a
  machine to show and sound; nothing in the match handles them.

## Collision

`collide.tide`, the game's own: see DESIGN.md for why it isn't Box3D (a
shape cast there can't leave out the body that casts it, or anything else).

- A map's colliders are boxes (`Piece`: center, rotation, half size, and a
  ball around it for a quick no). `NearBox` is the distance to one, exact
  outside and the nearest face's inside.
- `SweepBox` and `SweepCapsule` step a ball along its way by its distance to
  the shape, up to 40 steps, to within half a millimeter: the first touch,
  with its place and normal. A ball that starts inside is met at 0 with the
  normal against its way, which is what PhysX reports for such a sweep.
- A player's body is pushed out of each box its capsule is in, three rounds
  a step, and keeps none of its speed into the box, relative to how fast the
  box's surface goes there. It moves in steps of half its radius at the most
  (24 at the most a tick), so a dash at 60 m/s doesn't go through a wall.
- A mover's pieces are placed each tick from the mover (`PlacePieces`), so
  every question reads a piece's own `position` and `rotation`.

## Maps

`maps.tide` is generated by `tools/make_maps.py` from the Unity prefabs'
extracted data (a folder of JSON, one file a map, which the script's first
argument names; the extraction itself isn't in the repo). `BuildMap` fills
the match's lists and `BuildMapVisuals` what's drawn. On a mover or a block
a piece's `local` and a visual's `position` are from that mover's place (the
file's header says how for each kind). `--check` runs the generator's own
checks: 1,538 passed.

## What's drawn and heard

All local; nothing of it is in the match's hash.

- `render.tide`: the camera (the Cinemachine orbit's three rings, its
  composer offset and damping), which writes `Hand.yaw`, `pitch` and `aim`
  for the input, locks the cursor while the player plays (not while cards
  are dealt or a game is over) and sets the audio's listener; the map's
  blocks, an instanced draw a mesh, through a 2,048 pixel shadow map; the
  sky, a cubemap a map; the floor under the map as Grid.mat has it (black lines a meter apart with the sky between them, gone 20 m from the eye: the owner, 2026-10-11, of a first version that was a black plane: "it should be only a grid, almost like you are floating in the sky"); and the frame's bloom and grade.
- `mage.tide`: the Magician, skinned, with the Unity materials by slot (the
  model carries its slot in `uv.x`), the liquid in its head as its health,
  and its clips blended by how it moves.
- `effects.tide`: bullets, shields, clouds, sparks, damage numbers and the
  bars over other players.
- `hud.tide`, `cards.tide`: the player's HUD, the rounds, the cards (the
  picker's are `GUI.Control`s: a click, a tap, keys 1 to 5, or `GUI.Navigate`),
  game over, touch controls and the rooms menu's title page.
- `sound.tide`: a local handler for each event. Deaths, round results and
  cards taken wait for `verified`.

start and the seats; standing on all seven maps; walking, the air, the speed
cap, jumps, coyote time; firing, reloading, a bullet's flight, hits and

and jump pads; a test for each card with state of its own; input that's no
number; eight players doing anything for a minute; a player and a bot to the
end of a game; the same inputs twice, a snapshot, a packed world, and two
clients over a network with 40 ms of latency, 15 of jitter and 5% loss with
no world sent again.

- **The scripted match's hash is `0xd5f10d031789bc7a`**, the same natively
  and under WASI. Any change to the match changes it: update it from one
  platform and confirm the other.
- A test forces a map by writing `Arena.futureMap` in the two ticks after
  the joins, and a card by adding to `Arena.picks` then (`raise_map`, `give`).

- The pushable blocks stand still (DESIGN.md). That's the port's biggest gap
  from the Unity game: Map4 and Map6 are built around them.
- No browser playtest is in the suite. The web page was looked at as a title
  state on WebGL 2 with a software rasterizer; a round in a browser, WebGPU,
  and two machines through the relay weren't run.
- Nobody has played it: every check is a scripted run, a bot match or a
  capture the agent looked at. How it feels, on a mouse, a gamepad or a
  phone, is untested, and nobody has listened to it.
- Seen only through a debug view, or not at all: clouds, the lightning arc,
  the freeze ring, a jump pad's squash, the jetpack's flame, the dash's
  streak.
- Map10 seats a second player of the first team 0.32 m from a jump pad,
  inside its trigger; they aren't thrown, as they're in it from the spawn.
  Map7 seats one on a bridge's edge. Both are the Unity prefabs' places.

  decoded pixels. A release page compresses its pack.

## Engine gaps the game works around

Each was met as a compiler error or measured, not read in a doc.

1. **Box3D's shape casts can't leave anything out**, the caster included: a
   `Cast` from inside a body's own capsule meets that capsule at 0 (measured
   with a probe game). The Unity game's bullets and ground check each ask
   PhysX with a layer mask and skip their own body. So the port has its own
   collision, and no Box3D.
2. **Box3D has no mesh or hull shapes and no joints**, by its README. Not
   needed once collision was the game's own.
3. **A field of a vector in an array's element can't be assigned**:
   `arena.seats[s].velocity.y = 0` is an error ("a list's element is a
   copy"), though `arena.seats[s].velocity = ...` is written in place.
4. **A match event handler takes no query**, so `PlayerLeft` finds the
   player's entity through a map the match keeps.
5. **A local handler can't read the match's lists or maps.** `PadUsed` and
   `RoundStarted` leave a number in local state for a view to look up.
6. **A singleton's `Random` is seeded by the singleton's place in the
   program**, so adding a local singleton in another file changed the
   match's rolls. The game seeds its own from `Random.seed`.
7. **A function a frag calls can't take a singleton that holds a `Cubemap`
   or a `Random`**: the sky and the effects' dice are in singletons of their
   own.
8. **The pack keeps a `Cubemap`'s faces and a `Texture` as decoded pixels**
   whatever the source file weighs: seven skies at 512 pixels were 44 MB, so
   they're 256.
9. **A model's materials aren't imported**, so the mage's material slot is
   in its vertices.
10. **Separate animation files bind by bone names only**: Mixamo clips on a
    rig with other names were retargeted in Blender into the model's file.
11. **No custom fonts, no text wrapping, no texture size**: cards wrap their
    lines by measuring words, and icons are quads with UVs worked out by hand.
12. **A list can't hold a `Clip`** as a function's parameter, so a random
    pick from a glob of clips is written at each call.
13. **A package's page draws under the game's views**, so the rooms title
    page's art is a HUD view and the override keeps the buttons.
14. **A sound's volume over 1 clips.**
