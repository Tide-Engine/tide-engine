lookup: 4 systems each tick, 2 stages deep; 4 can run alongside others.
A system starts once everything it runs after is done.

stage 1  Aim  (per entity)
         writes Transform
         alongside Heal, Drain
stage 2  Chase  (per entity)
         reads Runner, Transform
         writes Transform
         after Aim: it writes Transform, which this reads
         alongside Heal, Drain
stage 1  Heal  (per entity)
         reads Healer
         writes Health
         alongside Aim, Chase
stage 2  Drain  (per entity)
         writes Health
         after Heal: both write Health
         alongside Aim, Chase

Event handlers, at the end of the tick, as their events are sent:
         Spawned: Setup
