overrides: 6 systems each tick, 3 stages deep; 6 can run alongside others.
A system starts once everything it runs after is done.

stage 1  Combat.Gravity  (per entity)
         writes Combat.Velocity
         alongside Combat.Count, Combat.Rest(Combat.Health, Combat.Log, with Combat.Player), Combat.Rest(Combat.Health, Combat.Log, without Combat.Player)
stage 1  Combat.Count  (once)
         writes Combat.Log
         alongside Combat.Gravity, Combat.Move, Combat.Clamp

stage 2  Combat.Move  (per entity)
         writes Combat.Position, Combat.Velocity
         after Combat.Gravity: both write Velocity
         alongside Combat.Count, Combat.Rest(Combat.Health, Combat.Log, with Combat.Player), Combat.Rest(Combat.Health, Combat.Log, without Combat.Player)
stage 2  Combat.Rest(Combat.Health, Combat.Log, with Combat.Player)  (per entity)
         writes Combat.Health, Combat.Log
         after Combat.Count: both write Log
         alongside Combat.Gravity, Combat.Move, Combat.Clamp

stage 3  Combat.Clamp  (per entity)
         writes Combat.Position
         after Combat.Move: both write Position
         alongside Combat.Count, Combat.Rest(Combat.Health, Combat.Log, with Combat.Player), Combat.Rest(Combat.Health, Combat.Log, without Combat.Player)
stage 3  Combat.Rest(Combat.Health, Combat.Log, without Combat.Player)  (per entity)
         reads Combat.Health
         writes Combat.Log
         after Combat.Count: both write Log (and through Combat.Rest(Combat.Health, Combat.Log, with Combat.Player))
         after Combat.Rest(Combat.Health, Combat.Log, with Combat.Player): both write Log
         alongside Combat.Gravity, Combat.Move, Combat.Clamp

Views, every frame in this order (they only read, so nothing waits):
         Combat.Show

Event handlers, at the end of the tick, as their events are sent:
         Spawned: Combat.Setup
