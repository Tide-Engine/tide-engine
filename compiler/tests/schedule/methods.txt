methods: 2 systems each tick, 2 stages deep; none can run at the same time.
A system starts once everything it runs after is done.

stage 1  Hurt  (per entity)
         writes Unit
         alongside nothing

stage 2  Tally  (per entity)
         reads Unit
         writes Log
         across threads: what its entities change of Log is changed after them, in their order
         after Hurt: it writes Unit, which this reads
         alongside nothing

Event handlers, at the end of the tick, as their events are sent:
         Spawned: Setup
