ordered: 6 systems each tick, 3 stages deep; 6 can run alongside others.
A system starts once everything it runs after is done.

stage 1  SpawnRocks  (per entity)
         reads Rock
         alongside RollDice, RollAgain, Fade
stage 1  RollDice  (per entity)
         writes Dice
         alongside SpawnRocks, SpawnSparks, SpawnWave, RollAgain, Fade
stage 1  RollAgain  (per entity)
         reads Time
         alongside SpawnRocks, SpawnSparks, SpawnWave, RollDice, Fade
stage 1  Fade  (per entity)
         reads Time
         writes Spark
         alongside SpawnRocks, SpawnSparks, SpawnWave, RollDice, RollAgain

stage 2  SpawnSparks  (per entity)
         reads Rock
         after SpawnRocks: both spawn, and entities get their IDs in order
         alongside RollDice, RollAgain, Fade

stage 3  SpawnWave  (once)
         writes Wave
         after SpawnRocks: both spawn, and entities get their IDs in order (and through SpawnSparks)
         after SpawnSparks: both spawn, and entities get their IDs in order
         alongside RollDice, RollAgain, Fade

Event handlers, at the end of the tick, as their events are sent:
         Spawned: Setup
