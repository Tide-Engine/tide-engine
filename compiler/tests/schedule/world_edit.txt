world_edit: 6 systems each tick, 3 stages deep; 6 can run alongside others.
A system starts once everything it runs after is done.

stage 1  Heal  (per entity)
         reads Healer
         writes any entity's Health, through World
         on one thread: it sets Health through its World, on any entity (world_edit.tide:42)
         alongside Rust, Tax, Count, Share
stage 1  Rust  (per entity)
         writes Armor
         alongside Heal, Drain, Tax, Count, Share
stage 1  Tax  (per entity)
         writes any entity's Gold, through World
         on one thread: it sets Gold through its World, on any entity (world_edit.tide:65)
         alongside Heal, Drain, Rust

stage 2  Drain  (per entity)
         writes Health
         after Heal: both write Health
         alongside Rust, Tax, Count, Share
stage 2  Count  (per entity)
         reads Gold
         after Tax: it writes Gold.value, which this reads
         alongside Heal, Drain, Rust

stage 3  Share  (per entity)
         writes Gold
         writes any entity's Gold, through World
         on one thread: it sets Gold through its World, on any entity (world_edit.tide:75)
         after Tax: both write Gold (and through Count)
         after Count: it reads Gold.value, which this writes
         alongside Heal, Drain, Rust

Event handlers, at the end of the tick, as their events are sent:
         Spawned: Setup
