grids: 4 systems each tick, 4 stages deep; none can run at the same time.
A system starts once everything it runs after is done.

stage 1  Paint  (once)
         writes Field
         alongside nothing

stage 2  Blur  (once, 1 parallel loop)
         writes Field
         after Paint: both change the text, lists or grids of Field; both write Field
         alongside nothing

stage 3  Fall  (once, 2 parallel loops)
         reads Time
         writes Field
         after Paint: both change the text, lists or grids of Field; both write Field (and through Blur)
         after Blur: both change the text, lists or grids of Field; both write Field
         alongside nothing

stage 4  Count  (once)
         reads Field
         writes Stats
         after Paint: it writes Field, which this reads (and through Blur)
         after Blur: it writes Field, which this reads (and through Fall)
         after Fall: it writes Field, which this reads
         alongside nothing

Parallel loops, and when their steps go to the threads:
         Blur, line 23: a grid's cells, a step reckoned at 86: on the threads from 1163 steps in a tick that's on them, 23256 in one that isn't
         Fall, line 32: a grid's cells, a step reckoned at 96: on the threads from 1042 steps in a tick that's on them, 20834 in one that isn't
         Fall, line 40: a grid's cells, a step reckoned at 42: on the threads from 2381 steps in a tick that's on them, 47620 in one that isn't
