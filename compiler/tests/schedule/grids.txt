grids: 6 systems each tick, 4 stages deep; 6 can run alongside others.
A system starts once everything it runs after is done.

stage 1  Paint  (once)
         writes Field
         alongside Generate, Bud
stage 1  Generate  (per entity)
         writes Chunk
         alongside Paint, Blur, Fall, Count

stage 2  Blur  (once, 1 parallel loop)
         writes Field
         after Paint: both change the text, lists or grids of Field; both write Field.cells
         alongside Generate, Bud
stage 2  Bud  (per entity)
         writes Chunk
         after Generate: both change the text, lists or grids of Chunk; both write Chunk.blocks
         alongside Paint, Blur, Fall, Count

stage 3  Fall  (once, 2 parallel loops)
         reads Time
         writes Field
         after Paint: both change the text, lists or grids of Field; both write Field.cells (and through Blur)
         after Blur: both change the text, lists or grids of Field; both write Field.cells
         alongside Generate, Bud

stage 4  Count  (once)
         reads Field
         writes Stats
         after Paint: it writes Field.cells, which this reads (and through Blur)
         after Blur: it writes Field.cells, which this reads (and through Fall)
         after Fall: it writes Field.cells, which this reads
         alongside Generate, Bud

Parallel loops, and when their steps go to the threads:
         Blur, line 23: a grid's cells, a step reckoned at 86: on the threads from 1163 steps in a tick that's on them, 23256 in one that isn't
         Fall, line 32: a grid's cells, a step reckoned at 96: on the threads from 1042 steps in a tick that's on them, 20834 in one that isn't
         Fall, line 40: a grid's cells, a step reckoned at 42: on the threads from 2381 steps in a tick that's on them, 47620 in one that isn't
