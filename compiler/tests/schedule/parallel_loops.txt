parallel_loops: 3 systems each tick, 3 stages deep; none can run at the same time.
A system starts once everything it runs after is done.

stage 1  Raise  (once, 1 parallel loop)
         writes Field
         alongside nothing

stage 2  Scale  (once, 1 parallel loop)
         writes Field
         after Raise: both change the text, lists or grids of Field; both write Field
         alongside nothing

stage 3  Tell  (once)
         reads Field
         after Raise: it writes Field, which this reads (and through Scale)
         after Scale: it writes Field, which this reads
         alongside nothing

Views, every frame in this order (they only read, so nothing waits):
         Place
         Near
         Fast

Event handlers, at the end of the tick, as their events are sent:
         Spawned: Setup

Local handlers of the match's events, at the start of a frame, for what the ticks since the last one sent:
         Poke: Heard

Loops that skip rows, and whether an index finds the rest:
         Place, line 40: not indexed: its guard puts no bound on a number of the row: compare one of the row's numbers, or its distance to a place, with a value the loop doesn't change

Parallel loops, and when their steps go to the threads:
         Raise, line 27: a list's elements, a step reckoned at 3008: on the threads from 34 steps in a tick that's on them, 665 in one that isn't
         Scale, line 35: a list's elements, on one thread: its steps read 'by', which lives on the thread that made it, as text, lists, maps, sets and grids in variables do
         Place, line 40: a query's rows, a step reckoned at 3017: on the threads from 663 steps a frame
         Heard, line 49: a list's elements, on one thread: a local handler's loops stay on its thread

Views that test an entity before copying anything of it:
         Place, the rows of its loop at line 40: 1 test, down to line 42
         Near: 2 tests, down to line 54
