# Art credits

## People

`people/walt.glb`, `ruth.glb`, `dale.glb`, `sam.glb` and `jo.glb` are from
Quaternius' Ultimate Modular Men and Ultimate Modular Women packs
(<https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html>,
<https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularwomen.html>), CC0 1.0. Each was
prepared by `people/tools/make_people.py` (Blender 4.4, headless) from the
pack's own file:

| File | Source | What changed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `walt.glb` | Men, `Farmer.blend` | Greyer brows, darker overalls and hat band |
| `ruth.glb` | Women, `Formal.gltf` | The green dress made a dusty blue house dress, her red hair grey |
| `dale.glb` | Men, `Casual_Hoodie.blend` | The purple hoodie a faded olive, dark shorts |
| `sam.glb` | Men, `Casual_2.blend` | A rust shirt, denim |
| `jo.glb` | Women, `Casual.gltf` | Denim for the orange trousers |

The script drops the packs' helper icosphere and any prop the character holds,
keeps the clips Idle, Idle_Neutral, Walk, Run, Interact and Wave, and exports
a GLB with the mirror modifiers applied.

## Props

The models in `props/` are from Kenney's Furniture Kit, Nature Kit, Survival
Kit and Car Kit (<https://kenney.nl/assets>), CC0 1.0, unchanged and named as
in the kits except `hatchback.glb` (the car kit's `hatchback-sports.glb`).
`car-colormap.png` and `survival-colormap.png` are the car and survival kits'
`Textures/colormap.png`.

## Sounds

Every sound in `../sfx/` was made by `../sfx/make_sfx.py` from noise and tones;
no recording is in them.
