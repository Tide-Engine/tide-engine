# GRAVEWAKE

Ashwick burned. Up to four survivors hold a wide, flat clearing in the wood
outside it, night after night, against endless and growing hordes of the
dead, thousands at once. It's seen from above, and plays like Risk of Rain 2
in a PSX-era picture:
each picks a survivor (a Gunner, a Breacher or an Engineer), a gun to aim and
three skills on cooldowns, and the dead never stop coming, more and tougher
the longer it goes and the further into the nights.

Each night, a beacon stands cold at one of the clearing's landmarks, a pale
column of wake-fire over it, and chests are hidden about the clearing. Look
about for them as long as you dare, then stand by the beacon to light it.
That starts the vigil: the dead come thicker, most of them for the beacon,
and the night's boss claws up, a mutant grown from the dead. Keep near the
beacon till it's full and its boss is down, and dawn breaks: the dead burn away, whoever's down or bled
out is up, and a moment later the next night falls, harder, at another
beacon. The chests not opened by then are gone with the night. While the
beacon's cold, optional missions come and go: do one in time and everyone
picks one of the rarer items.

Every kill leaves an orb of experience where the dead fell, worth more the
tougher they were, the party's once someone goes within a few metres of it;
at every level each takes one of three items that stack, most of which fight
on their own. How many nights, and how long, they held out is the score.

Play at <https://tide-engine.dev/prototypes/gravewake/>. **Play** is alone;
**Host a room** and send the code to fight with friends, up to four.

## Playing

| Action | Keyboard and mouse | Gamepad |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Walk up, down and across the screen; it's a run once you haven't fired for a moment | WASD | Left stick |
| Aim (the crosshair is the cursor) | Mouse | Right stick |
| The camera nearer or further | Mouse wheel | |
| Fire | Left mouse | Right trigger |
| Roll: a dodge, nothing the dead do touches you for most of it (two charges) | Space (or Shift, or right mouse) | East or South button, left trigger |
| Pick a survivor, take an item | 1, 2, 3 | D-pad left, up, right |
| Reload (it loads by itself when empty) | R | West button |
| The room's menu | Escape, or **Menu** | |

### The survivors

| Survivor | Gun | By itself | By itself |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Gunner | Carbine: automatic, through three | Phase Round: down the line with the most of the dead in it | Frag Grenade: into the thickest of them |
| Breacher | Pump Shotgun: eleven pellets, never needs loading; takes a third less from the dead | Boot: kicks the nearest close by over; one down is stamped on | Firebomb: into the thickest of them |
| Engineer | SMG: sets items off half again as often | Mine: laid as the dead come near | Turret: set down when the dead are about; fires on its own for fifteen seconds, setting off your items |

Everything but the gun and the roll happens by itself: each survivor's two
skills as soon as they're ready and the dead are in reach, and standing by a
chest opens it, by the beacon lights it, by a friend who's down raises them.
The guns never run out; only their magazines do.

The camera is high behind you looking down, north up the screen, and WASD
moves you up, down and across it whichever way you face; you turn to the
cursor, and your shots go level through it, or into the one of the dead it's
on: the cursor on a head is a headshot (the crosshair goes gold), on a
boss's weak spot a weak hit (orange). A ring on the ground marks each
survivor, and what stands between the camera and you is cut away. There's
no jumping.

### The nights

| | |
| --- | --- |
| The beacon | Five great braziers stand at the landmarks: in the cairn, in the chapel's ruin, by the graveyard, among the standing stones and by the dead oak. Each night one of them (never last night's) burns a pale wake-fire; a marker points to it, at the screen's edge when it's off it. Stand by it two and a half seconds to light it. |
| The chests | Four a night, hidden about the clearing and not marked; the night's panel says how many are found. Stand by one a moment and it opens: everyone picks one of three items, whoever found it. They're gone when the next night falls. |
| Missions | While the beacon's cold, every half a minute or so an optional mission appears, pointed to in gold: hold a circle (12 s with someone in it), cull the dead in one (25 of them, more for a party), or hunt down a marked elite, each within 45 to 60 s. Done, everyone picks one of three uncommon or rare items. Lighting the beacon calls off the one going. |
| The vigil | Lit, the beacon is kept while the survivors standing are within 11 m of it: in 90 s with all of them there, slower with only some, not at all with none. The dead come 1.4 times as fast, most of them rising round it. Lighting it also calls back anyone who bled out. |
| Its boss | One rises as it's lit (two from the fourth night, three from the seventh), the nights taking the three in turn; dawn doesn't come till they're down. Felled, each gives everyone an item to pick. |
| Dawn | Everything left of the dead burns away, every orb flies in, everyone's up, whole and ready. Ten seconds later the next night falls: more dangerous than the last (the danger climbs with the clock, and each night's is 1.25 times the one before), and each of its kind (mist, a blood moon, starless, the wake). |
| Waiting | A night that goes on more than four and a half minutes grows impatient (IMPATIENT on its panel): the danger climbs twice as fast till its dawn. While it's unlit, a boss comes every two and a half minutes, with no prize. |

### The items

At every level, and for every one of a night's bosses felled, three to choose
from (1, 2 or 3, shown across the middle of the screen); each stacks. The run
waits while anyone chooses. A chest gives everyone a pick too.

| Rarity | Items |
| --- | --- |
| Common | Hollow Points (damage), Hair Trigger (fire and reload faster), Kevlar Vest (health, armour), Running Shoes (speed), Leech Rounds (kills heal), Field Battery (skills back sooner), Extended Mag, Lucky Shot (hits for double), Smelling Salts (raise friends faster; not offered alone) |
| Uncommon | Live Wire (hits arc to more of the dead), Powder Keg (kills burst into the two nearest dead), Gun Drone (shoots the nearest dead), Ember Ring (sets the dead near you alight), Frostbite (hits freeze), Energy Drink (another roll), Kerosene (the dead you roll past catch fire), Steel Toes (more to the dead on the ground), Gas Mask (Bloaters' bursts can't hurt you; the ones you kill burst wider), AP Rounds (through one more) |
| Rare | Mortar Flare (hits call a shell down), Second Wind (going down, straight back up, used up), HE Rounds (every round bursts where it lands), Dragon's Breath (hits set the dead alight) |

Uncommon and rare items turn up more as the run goes on. What you take is
yours till the game's over, through going down and bleeding out.

### The dead

They claw up out of the ground, three a second at first (more with more of
you) and many more as the danger climbs (Easy, Medium, Hard, Very Hard,
Insane, Impossible), tougher and quicker too, up to 4000 at once. Most come
in hordes: every five to nine seconds the ones due claw up together out of
one patch of ground 22 to 32 m from one of you (or, in a vigil, mostly from
the beacon), a groan going up from them, all at one pace; a quarter come
alone, from anywhere. A head hit is gold on the crosshair, a Lucky Shot's
double yellow. Every one pressed against you slows you; a roll goes through
them. Their claws hit harder as the danger climbs, and your health comes back only
slowly, once you've gone four seconds unhurt.

| Kind | From | |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bloater | 40 s | Wider, slower, sick green with glowing boils, two and a half times as tough. Within 2.4 m of you it swells and bursts a second later, hurting everyone within 3.6 m (up to 45) and tearing up the dead round it. Shoot it dead and it bursts at once: from far off, that's a weapon. |
| Spitter | 75 s | Thin and sallow, its throat glowing. It hangs back, 4 to 13 m off, and with a clear line to you its throat swells and it lobs bile that hurts (up to 18) and slows you for 2 s. Find it and shoot it. |
| Warden | 2 min | Broad, in dark iron mail, its head bare. Rounds, blasts and blows to its body do a quarter as much (a grey mark, and they ring); its head takes them all. On its back, knocked down, its mail doesn't help, and fire burns through it. Its claws hit half again as hard. |

### The bosses

Mutants twice the size of the rest, their name, health and weak spot across
the top of the screen. A round anywhere into their bulk strikes them, feet
and all. Each has a growth that glows: a round there does several times as
much (an orange mark on the crosshair), and it glows brighter when it's
open. While one stands, fewer of the dead come. Roll through their slams,
charges and leaps.

| Boss | | Weak spot |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The Mauler | A hulk with arms like trunks. It slams (a red ring on the ground is where it comes down) and charges (a line on the ground is where it'll run); after a charge it stands spent a moment. | The sores on its back: three times as much |
| The Broodmother | A great egg sac on spindly legs, slow. She lobs a spread of bile and crouches to birth a pack of runners. | Her sac: twice as much, four times while she births |
| The Stalker | Stilt-legged, long-necked, quick. It crouches and leaps at you (a ring is where it lands), swipes close in, and shrieks, sending the dead near it into a frenzy. | The sac at its throat: half again as much, four times while it shrieks; enough there cuts the shriek short and staggers it |

Down, you fight on from the ground with a pistol, and a friend standing by
you for three seconds raises you, the gun back in hand as loaded as it was. Alone,
going down is the end. Bleed out and you're gone till the beacon's lit or
dawn breaks, watching a friend over their shoulder; then you're back beside
the beacon with everything you had.

Development keys, not in play's interface: F2 six of the dead of a kind
(Bloaters, Spitters, Wardens in turn), F3 a boss (the Mauler, the Broodmother,
the Stalker in turn), F4 two thousand more of the
dead, F5 a level (and so an offer), F6 walk to the next part of the clearing,
F7 the night kept (dawn now), F9 a camera over the clearing, F10 a random
item, F1 a mission (a hold, a cull, a hunt in turn).

## Ashwick Wood

About 125 m across, its ground near flat, rising into the trees at its edge,
and open: little stands in the way between the landmarks. The dead claw up
out of the ground all round you, 16 to 32 m off, from every side.

| Where | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| The middle | The camp's fire, where everyone starts; worn paths out to each landmark |
| North | The chapel's ruin, its walls half down (a beacon in it) |
| East | The cairn: a ring of great boulders open toward the fire, a beacon in it |
| West | The old graveyard: ten graves the dead rise from, their stones small enough to run past; a beacon at its edge |
| North-west | A ring of standing stones, a beacon in it |
| South | A great dead oak, a beacon beside it |
| About the clearing | A few sparse groves and big stones to stand behind |

## Build and run

From the repository root:

```sh
cmake --preset debug -DTIDE_PRIVATE_PROTOTYPES=ON
cmake --build --preset debug --target gravewake gravewake_test
ctest --preset debug -R '^gravewake'
```

For the browser page, and the browser test on both backends:

```sh
cmake --preset web-debug -DTIDE_PRIVATE_PROTOTYPES=ON
cmake --build --preset web-debug --target gravewake_page gravewake_test
ctest --preset web-debug -R '^gravewake'
```

Serve `docs/static` and visit `/prototypes/gravewake/`. Or run `tide run` in
this folder.
