# RocketSim attribution

Aerial's car, ball and contact code is a separate implementation in Tide, with
no code copied or translated line for line. It follows the behavior, the order
of a car's update and many of the constants of RocketSim, the open
reimplementation of Rocket League's physics, and two of its tests hold it to
numbers RocketSim produces (revision
c2baacb8f4b441dd8505e63c2aeb5a1679b60b02, as recorded in
`prototypes/rocket-league/RESEARCH.md`).
Source: https://github.com/ZealanL/RocketSim
