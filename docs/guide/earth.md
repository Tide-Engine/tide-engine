# Earth demo

Walk or drive around a little planet with everyone else playing. Visit its towns, roads, gentle hills and different biomes. The demo loads only when you choose **Play Earth**.

<div data-tide-earth></div>

## Controls

Click or tap the planet to land. Drag to turn the globe or camera. On the ground, use **WASD** to move, **C** to switch between walking and driving, **Space** to jump and **E** to wave. Tap the ground to go there; touch buttons provide the other actions. Use **Esc** or **Globe** to return to space.

Tap the **FPS line** at bottom right, or press **F8**, to open the engine's performance stats. **Expand** gives the game the whole page without restarting it. **Stop** leaves the match and releases the demo's resources.

The demo joins the shared hosted world when available; otherwise it starts a local world. Its [source is on GitHub](https://github.com/Tide-Engine/tide-engine/tree/dev/examples/earth).
