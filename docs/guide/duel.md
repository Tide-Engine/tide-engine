# Duel

A small 2D arena game inspired by Rounds, made with Tide. Jump, aim, shoot and block, then build a stronger fighter between rounds. Click the game to give it the mouse and keyboard.

## Play

Each match starts with a blue fighter facing an orange practice bot. A friend who joins takes the orange fighter's place. Win five rounds to win the match. After each loss, the losing fighter picks one of three upgrades, which stays with them for the rest of the match.

Block briefly to reflect incoming shots. Your shield takes two seconds to recharge. Pick upgrades by clicking or tapping a card, or pressing `1`, `2` or `3`.

| Action | Mouse and keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Move | `A` / `D`, or left / right arrows | Left stick |
| Jump | Space, `W`, or up arrow | South face button |
| Aim | Mouse | Right stick |
| Fire | Left mouse button | Right trigger |
| Block | Right mouse button or Shift | Left trigger or left shoulder |

### Touch controls

| Action | Touchscreen |
|---|---|
| Move | Drag left or right on the left half |
| Jump | Drag upward with the same finger |
| Aim and fire | Hold a finger on the right half |
| Block | Tap the block area in the bottom right |

To jump again, lower your finger or lift it and start a new upward swipe.

## Duel a friend

Press **Invite** to open a room. Its code appears underneath; press **Copy** to put it on the clipboard. Your friend opens this page, pastes the code into the field beside **Join** (Ctrl+V, or Cmd+V), and presses **Join**. They take the orange fighter's place.

Joining or leaving starts a fresh match. If your friend leaves, the practice bot takes over their fighter.

Both machines run the same arena from the players' inputs.
